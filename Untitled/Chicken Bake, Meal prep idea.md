# Chicken Enchilada Rice Bake

**Serves:** 6  
**Prep:** ~10–15 minutes  
**Cook:** ~55–60 minutes  
**Style:** One-dish chicken and rice casserole

> Tender chicken, jasmine rice, black beans, corn, peppers, and green chiles baked together in mild red enchilada sauce and chicken broth, then finished with melted Mexican-blend cheese.

## Ingredients

### Chicken & Rice Bake
- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1½ cups jasmine rice, uncooked
- 1 can (10 oz) mild red enchilada sauce
- 1¾ cups chicken broth, warmed
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup corn, drained
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 small can mild diced green chiles
- 2 Tbsp minced garlic
- 100 g reduced-fat Mexican-blend cheese

### Seasoning Blend
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

### Toppings
- Your favorite toppings, optional

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat the oven
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F**.
2. Lightly grease a **9 × 13-inch baking dish** if desired.

### 2. Assemble the bake
1. Add the chicken, uncooked jasmine rice, diced onion, bell pepper, corn, black beans, green chiles, and minced garlic directly to the baking dish.
2. Add the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
3. Pour the enchilada sauce and warm chicken broth over everything.
4. Stir gently to distribute the ingredients and seasonings.
5. Spread everything into an even layer.
6. Make sure the rice is submerged beneath the liquid.

### 3. Bake
1. Cover the baking dish tightly with foil.
2. Bake at **400°F for 45 minutes**.

### 4. Add the cheese
1. Remove the foil.
2. Sprinkle the reduced-fat Mexican-blend cheese evenly over the top.
3. Return the dish to the oven **uncovered**.
4. Bake for another **10–15 minutes**, until the cheese is melted, the rice is tender, and the chicken reaches an internal temperature of **165°F**.

### 5. Rest
1. Remove the baking dish from the oven.
2. Let it rest for **10 minutes**.
3. This allows the rice to absorb any remaining liquid.

### 6. Serve
1. Divide into **6 servings**.
2. Add your favorite toppings.
3. Serve hot and enjoy!

## Notes

- **Cheese discrepancy:** The ingredient list specifies **100 g**, while the original directions specify **150 g**. This version uses **100 g**.
- Make sure the rice is submerged in the liquid before covering and baking.
- Use a meat thermometer to confirm the chicken reaches **165°F**.
- Letting the casserole rest for 10 minutes is important for the rice to finish absorbing the liquid.
- This should keep well as **6 meal-prep portions** in the refrigerator.
- Good topping options: avocado, sour cream, Greek yogurt, cilantro, sliced green onion, jalapeño, salsa, or hot sauce.
