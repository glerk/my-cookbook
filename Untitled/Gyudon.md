# Gyudon (牛丼)

**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** ~5 minutes  
**Cook:** ~15 minutes  
**Style:** Japanese beef rice bowl

> Thinly sliced beef and onions simmered in a savory-sweet sauce, then served over hot rice with pickled red ginger.

## Ingredients

### Beef Bowl
- 1 lb thinly sliced beef (ribeye or chuck)
- 1 onion
- 2 green onions/scallions
- Cooked Japanese short-grain rice
- Pickled red ginger, for garnish

### Sauce
- 1 cup dashi
- 4 Tbsp sake
- 4 Tbsp mirin
- 6 Tbsp soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp sugar, or to taste

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the ingredients
1. Thinly slice the onion.
2. Slice the green onions.
3. Cut the beef into smaller pieces if needed.

### 2. Make the sauce
1. Add the dashi, sake, mirin, soy sauce, and sugar to a cold frying pan.
2. Stir to combine.

### 3. Simmer
1. Add the onion to the pan.
2. Place the beef on top.
3. Cover and turn the heat to medium.
4. Once simmering, reduce to low and cook for 3–4 minutes.
5. Skim off any scum or fat if needed.
6. Add the green onions and cook for 1 more minute.

### 4. Serve
1. Spoon hot rice into bowls.
2. Top with the beef and onion mixture.
3. Add pickled red ginger.
4. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Use well-marbled beef if possible.
- Don’t overcook the beef.
- Dashi powder can be used in a pinch.
- For no alcohol, replace sake with water.
