# Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Makes:** Varies by cookie size (50–60 g each)  
**Prep:** 15 minutes  
**Chill:** At least 2 hours  
**Cook:** 13–14 minutes per batch  
**Total:** Approximately 2 hours 30 minutes, plus baking additional batches  
**Style:** American

> Soft, thick, bakery-style chocolate chip cookies with chewy centers, lightly browned edges, and plenty of chocolate.

---

## Ingredients

### Main Ingredients

- 2¼ cups (281 g) all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1½ tsp cornstarch
- ½ tsp salt
- ¾ cup (170 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled for 5 minutes
- ¾ cup (150 g) packed light or dark brown sugar
- ½ cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1 large egg yolk, room temperature
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1¼ cups (225 g) semi-sweet chocolate chips or chunks

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Dry Ingredients

1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, cornstarch, and salt.
2. Set aside.

### 2. Mix the Wet Ingredients

1. In a medium bowl, whisk melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth.
2. Add the egg and egg yolk. Whisk until combined.
3. Stir in vanilla extract.

### 3. Make the Dough

1. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients.
2. Mix with a large spoon or spatula until combined.
3. Fold in chocolate chips.
4. The dough will be very soft, thick, and shiny. The chocolate chips may not stick easily because of the melted butter, but keep mixing.

### 4. Chill the Dough

1. Cover the bowl tightly.
2. Refrigerate for at least **2 hours**, preferably overnight.
3. Dough can be refrigerated for up to 3 days.
4. If chilled longer than 2 hours, let the dough sit at room temperature for about 15 minutes before shaping.

**Important:** Don't skip chilling! It helps prevent excessive spreading and produces thicker cookies.

### 5. Shape the Cookies

1. Preheat oven to **325°F (163°C)**.
2. Line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.
3. Scoop the chilled dough:
   - XL cookies: 3 scant tablespoons (60 g)
   - Medium-large cookies: 2 heaping tablespoons (50 g)
4. Roll each portion into a ball.
5. Shape each ball into a tall cylinder rather than a wide, flat ball.
6. Arrange cookies approximately 3 inches apart.

### 6. Bake

1. Bake for **13–14 minutes**, until the edges are very lightly browned.
2. The centers will look very soft. This is normal; they will continue setting as they cool.
3. Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes.
4. Transfer to a cooling rack.
5. Optional: Press a few extra chocolate chips into the tops while the cookies are still warm.

---

## Important Notes

- **Don't skip chilling:** This is essential for thick cookies that don't spread excessively.
- **Shape matters:** Tall, cylindrical dough portions help create thick, bakery-style cookies.
- **Don't overbake:** The centers should still look soft when removed from the oven.
- **Cornstarch:** Helps keep cookies soft and thick. It can be omitted if necessary, although the texture will be slightly different.
- **Room-temperature eggs:** If your eggs are cold, place them in a bowl of warm water for 5 minutes.

## Variations

- Substitute chocolate chunks for chocolate chips.
- Add chopped nuts, M&Ms
