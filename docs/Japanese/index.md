# 🍜 Japanese Food

A collection of Japanese recipes, including dishes we've learned, favorites we've discovered, and recipes inspired by our time in Japan.

---

## Recipes

* [Gyudon (牛丼)](gyudon.md)
  Japanese beef and rice bowl. This similar to what they have a Yoshinoya (吉野家). I used to eat this when I lived in Nagoya! 

* [Nagoya Miso Katsu (味噌カツ)](miso-katsu.md)
  Crispy pork cutlet with a rich Nagoya-style miso sauce. This is a Nagoya Classic. I would frequent Misokatsu Yabaton restaurant! Soo gooood.
