## Instructions — Shrimp Version

### 1. Prepare

- Preheat oven to **400°F**.
- Thaw shrimp completely if frozen.
- Peel and devein if necessary, then pat dry with paper towels.
- Place the Boursin in the center of a **9×13 baking dish**.
- Add the cherry tomatoes, sliced shallot, and minced garlic around the Boursin.
- Drizzle everything with olive oil.
- Sprinkle with oregano, smoked paprika, onion powder, red pepper flakes, and salt.

### 2. Bake the Tomatoes and Boursin

- Bake for **20–25 minutes**, until the tomatoes have softened and begun to burst.
- While baking, cook the rotini according to package directions until **al dente**.
- Drain and reserve a little pasta water.

### 3. Prepare the Creamy Sauce

- Remove the baking dish from the oven.
- Squeeze the lemon juice over everything.
- Burst the softened tomatoes with a spoon.
- Stir the tomatoes, melted Boursin, garlic, and seasonings together until a creamy sauce forms.

### 4. Cook the Shrimp

- Add the raw shrimp directly to the baking dish.
- Toss gently to coat the shrimp in the sauce.
- Spread the shrimp into an even layer.
- Return the dish to the oven for approximately **6–10 minutes**, depending on shrimp size.
- Shrimp are done when opaque throughout and they reach an internal temperature of **145°F**.

**Important:** Don't overcook the shrimp! They can become rubbery very quickly. Start checking around 6 minutes.

### 5. Add Pasta and Spinach

- Remove the baking dish from the oven.
- Add the cooked rotini and baby spinach.
- Gently toss everything together until the pasta is coated and the spinach has wilted.
- If the sauce is too thick, add a small splash of reserved pasta water.

### 6. Portion and Serve

- Divide evenly between **6 meal-prep containers**.
- Top with fresh parsley if desired.
- Allow to cool slightly before refrigerating.

---

## Shrimp Version Notes

- **Shrimp:** Use approximately 24 oz (1½ lbs) raw, peeled, deveined large shrimp. Frozen shrimp should be completely thawed and patted dry.
- **Cooking time:** Shrimp cooks much faster than salmon. Always add it after the tomatoes and Boursin have baked.
- **Size:** Large or jumbo shrimp work best. Smaller shrimp may cook in less time.
- **Meal prep:** Reheat gently in the microwave in short intervals, stirring between intervals, to avoid overcooking the shrimp.
- **Storage:** Refrigerate in airtight containers and use within 3–4 days.
