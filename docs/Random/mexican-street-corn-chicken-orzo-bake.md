# Mexican Street Corn Chicken Orzo Bake

**Serves:** 6  
**Prep Time:** ~15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** ~40 minutes  
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes  
**Style:** One-dish chicken and orzo bake / Mexican street corn-inspired

> Creamy, cheesy chicken orzo loaded with Mexican-style street corn, green chiles, lime, cilantro, cotija, and Tajín. Easy one-dish meal prep with big street-corn flavor.

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat Oven

Preheat oven to **400°F**.

### 2. Assemble the Bake

Add the following to a deep **9×13-inch baking dish**:

- Dry orzo
    
- Diced raw chicken
    
- Drained Mexican-style street corn
    
- Diced red onion
    
- Minced garlic
    
- Diced green chiles
    
- Olive oil
    
- Street corn seasoning blend
    
- Chicken broth
    

Mix everything together well, making sure the orzo is mostly submerged in the broth.

### 3. Bake

Cover the baking dish tightly with foil.

Bake for approximately **40 minutes**, or until:

- The orzo is tender
    
- The chicken reaches an internal temperature of **165°F**
    

### 4. Make It Creamy

Remove from the oven.

While everything is still hot, stir in:

- Softened cream cheese
    
- **Half of the cotija cheese**
    
- Lime juice
    

Stir until the cream cheese is completely melted and the mixture is smooth and creamy.

Add Tajín to taste.

### 5. Add Cilantro

Stir in most of the chopped cilantro, reserving some for topping.

### 6. Finish

Top with:

- Remaining cotija cheese
    
- Reserved cilantro
    
- Additional Tajín, if desired
    

### 7. Meal Prep

Divide evenly into **6 meal-prep containers**.

Before eating, finish each serving with an **extra squeeze of fresh lime** to really make the street-corn flavor pop. 🔥

## Notes

- **Tajín:** Don't be shy. The lime/chile/salt flavor is a big part of the street-corn profile. Measure with your heart.
    
- **Chicken:** Cut the chicken into relatively small, evenly sized pieces so it cooks evenly with the orzo.
    
- **Cream cheese:** Softened cream cheese will incorporate much more easily into the hot orzo.
    
- **Cotija:** Reserve half for topping so you get both creamy, salty flavor throughout and the characteristic cotija texture on top.
    
- **Cayenne:** Optional. Tajín already provides some chile flavor, so adjust based on how spicy you want it.
    
- **Lime:** The extra squeeze of lime immediately before eating is highly recommended.
    
- **Scaling:** The serving calculator scales ingredient amounts, but the baking dish size and cooking time may need adjustment if you make a substantially larger or smaller batch.
