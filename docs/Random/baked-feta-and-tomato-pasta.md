# Baked Feta and Tomato Pasta

**Serves:** 2  
**Prep Time:** ~10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** ~30 minutes  
**Total Time:** ~40 minutes  
**Style:** Mediterranean-inspired pasta

> Roasted cherry tomatoes and creamy baked feta tossed with garlic, olive oil, oregano, fresh basil, and pasta.

---

## Ingredients

### Baked Tomatoes and Feta

- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="200" data-unit="g">200 g</span> cherry tomatoes
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="200" data-unit="g">200 g</span> feta cheese, preferably one block
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="2" data-unit="Tbsp">2 Tbsp</span> chopped garlic
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="0.333333" data-unit="cup">⅓ cup</span> extra-virgin olive oil
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="3" data-unit="Tbsp">3 Tbsp</span> dried oregano, divided
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="0.5" data-unit="tsp">½ tsp</span> black pepper
- Salt, to taste

### Pasta and Fresh Herbs

- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="4" data-unit="oz">4 oz</span> pasta of choice (capellini works well)
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="0.25" data-unit="cup">¼ cup</span> fresh basil leaves, roughly chopped

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Oven and Tomatoes

1. Preheat the oven to **200°C / 400°F**.
2. Add the cherry tomatoes and chopped garlic to an oven-proof baking dish.
3. Pour in half of the olive oil and mix well.
4. Add a pinch of salt, the black pepper, and **2 Tbsp of the oregano**. Stir to combine.

### 2. Add the Feta

1. Place the whole block of feta in the center of the dish, surrounded by the tomatoes.
2. Pour the remaining olive oil over the feta.
3. Sprinkle the remaining **1 Tbsp oregano** over the feta.
4. Avoid adding much additional salt at this stage because feta is naturally salty. You can adjust the seasoning after mixing in the pasta.

### 3. Bake

Bake uncovered for approximately **30 minutes**, until the tomatoes have softened and burst and the feta is soft.

### 4. Cook the Pasta

While the tomatoes and feta bake:

1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
3. Drain the pasta. If desired, reserve a little pasta water to loosen the sauce later.

### 5. Make the Sauce

1. Remove the baking dish from the oven and let it rest for **5 minutes**.
2. Use a spoon to mix the feta and roasted tomatoes together until creamy.
3. Stir in the chopped fresh basil.

### 6. Combine and Serve

1. Add the cooked pasta to the baking dish.
2. Toss until the pasta is evenly coated in the feta and tomato sauce.
3. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper as needed.
4. If the sauce is too thick, stir in a small splash of reserved pasta water.
5. Serve warm.

---

## Notes

- **Oregano:** The recipe uses 3 Tbsp total: 2 Tbsp with the tomatoes and 1 Tbsp over the feta.
- **Feta:** A block of feta generally mixes more smoothly than pre-crumbled feta.
- **Pasta:** Capellini cooks quickly, so check the package directions and avoid overcooking it.
- **Salt:** Feta is salty, so taste the finished sauce before adding more.
- **Garlic:** The original recipe calls for 2 Tbsp chopped garlic. This is more than a couple of cloves; measure it as written or adjust to your taste.
- **Sauce consistency:** A little reserved pasta water can help the sauce cling to the pasta.
