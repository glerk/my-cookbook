# Taco Rice

**Serves:** 4  
**Prep:** ~5 minutes  
**Cook:** ~15 minutes

> Chicken, taco seasoning, rice, corn, and tomato mix cooked together in one pan.

<div class="servings-calculator" data-original-servings="4"></div>


---


## Ingredients

### Main
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="3" data-unit="Chicken breasts" data-unit-one="Chicken breasts" data-unit-many="chicken breasts">3 Chicken breasts</span> cut up into bite size chunks
- Oil, for cooking
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="1.5" data-unit="bag">1½ bags</span> taco seasoning
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="1" data-unit="can">1 can</span> corn
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="1" data-unit="can">1 can</span> Rotel Mexican tomato mix
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="1" data-unit="can">1 can</span> tomato sauce
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="2" data-unit="tbsp">2 tbsp</span> garlic, minced
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-amount="1.5" data-unit="cup">1½ cups</span> uncooked rice
- <span class="ingredient-amount" data-min="1" data-max="2" data-unit-one="cup" data-unit-many="cups">1–2 cups</span> chicken broth

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the chicken
1. Heat oil in a pan.
2. Add the cubed chicken.
3. Cook until the edges are browned.

### 2. Season
1. Add the taco seasoning.
2. Mix well.
3. Cook for about 1 minute.

### 3. Add the rest
1. Add the corn.
2. Add the rice.
3. Add the Rotel.
4. Add the tomato sauce.
5. Add the garlic.
6. Pour in the chicken broth.

### 4. Finish
1. Stir every few minutes so it does not stick.
2. Cook for about 15 minutes, or until the liquid is gone and the mixture is thick.

## Notes
- Serves about 3–4 people.
- Serve over rice if desired.
- Goes good with chips or tostada shells. 
- Can be put into a burrito. 
- Top it with some sour cream or guac
