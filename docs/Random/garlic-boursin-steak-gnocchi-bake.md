# Garlic Boursin Steak & Gnocchi Bake

**Servings:** 6 meals  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 40–45 minutes  
**Oven:** 425°F, then 400°F

A hearty one-pan meal with tender gnocchi, roasted tomatoes, mushrooms, and a creamy Garlic & Fine Herbs Boursin sauce, topped with sliced seared steak and melted cheese.

## Ingredients

### Steak

- 1.5 lbs boneless petite sirloin steak
    
- Remaining half of the seasoning blend
    

### Gnocchi Bake

- 20 oz shelf-stable potato gnocchi, uncooked
    
- 20 oz cherry tomatoes
    
- 1 package (5.2 oz) Garlic & Fine Herbs Boursin cheese
    
- 8 oz baby bella mushrooms, sliced
    
- 1 small shallot, finely diced
    
- 2 cups fresh baby spinach
    
- 1/4 cup shredded mozzarella
    
- 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan
    
- 1 tbsp olive oil
    
- Fresh parsley, finely chopped (for garnish)
    

### Seasoning Blend

- 2 tsp garlic powder
    
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning
    
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
    
- 1 tsp onion powder
    
- 1 tsp salt
    
- 1 tsp black pepper
    

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the oven and baking dish:** Preheat oven to 425°F. Place the Boursin cheese in the center of a 9×13-inch baking dish. Arrange the uncooked gnocchi, cherry tomatoes, mushrooms, and diced shallot around it.
    
2. **Season the vegetables and gnocchi:** Drizzle everything with olive oil. Combine all seasoning blend ingredients and sprinkle half over the gnocchi and vegetables.
    
3. **Roast:** Bake uncovered for 30–35 minutes, stirring the gnocchi and vegetables halfway through, until the tomatoes have burst and the gnocchi is tender with lightly golden edges.
    
4. **Cook the steak:** While the gnocchi bakes, season both sides of the petite sirloin steaks with the remaining seasoning blend. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and sear for approximately 3–5 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness and desired doneness.
    
5. **Rest and slice:** Remove the steaks from the skillet and let them rest for 5–10 minutes. Slice thinly against the grain.
    
6. **Make the sauce:** Remove the baking dish from the oven. Mash the roasted tomatoes and softened Boursin together until a creamy sauce forms. Stir to coat the gnocchi evenly.
    
7. **Add spinach and steak:** Stir in the fresh spinach until it begins to wilt. Add the sliced steak and gently mix everything together.
    
8. **Add the cheese:** Sprinkle the mozzarella and Parmesan evenly over the top. Reduce oven temperature to 400°F and return the dish to the oven for 5–8 minutes, until the cheese melts.
    
9. **Brown the top:** Finish under the broiler for 1–2 minutes, watching closely until the cheese is golden brown.
    
10. **Serve:** Garnish with fresh parsley. Divide evenly into 6 meal-prep portions.
    

## Notes

- **Steak doneness:** Use an instant-read thermometer for accuracy. For whole beef steaks, USDA guidance is 145°F followed by a 3-minute rest. Adjust cooking time based on steak thickness.
    
- **Gnocchi texture:** Shelf-stable gnocchi can vary by brand. Check tenderness before removing from the oven.
    
- **Broiling:** Keep a close eye on the cheese because it can brown quickly.
    

## Meal Prep & Storage

- Divide into 6 airtight containers.
    
- Refrigerate for up to 3–4 days.
    
- Reheat in the microwave, stirring halfway through. Add a small splash of water if the sauce has thickened.
