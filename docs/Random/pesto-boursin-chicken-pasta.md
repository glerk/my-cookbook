# Creamy Pesto Boursin Chicken Pasta Meal Prep

**Serves:** 6
**Prep Time:** ~15 minutes
**Cook Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Style:** Meal Prep

> Juicy chicken, bow tie pasta, roasted tomatoes, zucchini, garlic, pesto, and Boursin cheese tossed together into a creamy, flavorful pasta meal prep.

---

## Ingredients

### Main Ingredients

* 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breast
* 8 oz bow tie pasta
* 1 (5.3 oz) Boursin Garlic & Fine Herbs cheese
* 2 pints cherry tomatoes
* 1 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
* 1 medium shallot, thinly sliced
* 6 garlic cloves, peeled
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* ⅓ cup pesto
* 2 cups fresh spinach
* ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan
* Juice of ½ lemon
* ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped

### Seasoning Blend

**For the vegetables:**

* 1 tsp Italian seasoning
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper
* ½ tsp red pepper flakes, optional

**For the chicken:**

* 1 tbsp smoked paprika
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Vegetables

* Preheat oven to **400°F**.
* Place the Boursin in the center of a **9×13 baking dish**.
* Add the cherry tomatoes, zucchini, shallot, and garlic cloves.
* Drizzle with olive oil.
* Season with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes.

### 2. Roast

* Bake uncovered for **30–35 minutes**, until the tomatoes are blistered and the vegetables are softened.

### 3. Cook the Chicken

* While the vegetables are roasting, cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
* Season with smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
* Heat a skillet over **medium-high heat**.
* Cook the chicken for approximately **7–10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through.
